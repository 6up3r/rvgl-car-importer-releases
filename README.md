## 🆕 New in v0.400

<b>Twelve new games</b>, all on PlayStation: Destruction Derby, Destruction Derby 2 and Destruction Derby Raw, TOCA Touring Car Championship, TOCA 2 Touring Cars and TOCA World Touring Cars, Driver and Driver 2, Ridge Racer, Ridge Racer High Spec Ver., Ridge Racer Revolution and R4: Ridge Racer Type 4. Twenty-seven games in all, over 1,300 cars in over 2,300 versions.

- A floor under every body: Destruction Derby, the Ridge Racers and R4 no longer show the inside of their shell when they roll over.
- Tyres and wheel wells keep their colour in RVGL instead of taking the colour of the level's lights.
- The new games' engines raised to Gran Turismo 2's loudness, where they played too quietly in RVGL.
- RC Revenge and RC Revenge Pro: bodies and wheels lit from the right side.

Every change is in the [release notes](https://github.com/6up3r/rvgl-car-importer-releases/releases/tag/v0.400).

## 🛠️ Reporting a problem

1,010 of Gran Turismo 2's 1,043 versions are checked against the game and 33 are still under study, and nobody can check that many cars by hand in every detail: your reports are how they get fixed. Before reporting:

- update the app (**File > Check for updates...**): the problem may already be fixed;
- look at the car in the app: a purple **REPORTED** tag means its problem is already known, and the issue is linked under the car's sheet. Report it again only if yours is a different problem.

### 🚗 A car looks or drives wrong

1. Open the Gran Turismo 2 car in the app and press **Report a problem...** next to its name (the button is greyed out on the other games' cars).
2. The [car report form](https://github.com/6up3r/rvgl-car-importer-releases/issues/new?template=car.yml) opens with the car, the disc and the app's version already filled in.
3. Choose the category, say where it shows (in the app or in RVGL), describe what you see and attach a screenshot.

One report per car and per problem: two problems on one car are two reports.

### 💥 The app closed unexpectedly

1. Start the app again: it tells you where it saved the crash report.
2. Press **Report on GitHub**: the [crash report form](https://github.com/6up3r/rvgl-car-importer-releases/issues/new?template=crash.yml) and the report's folder open.
3. Drag the report file into the form and say what you were doing when it closed.

The report holds the app's version, the system and the error; your home folder is written `~`, so it carries no user name.

**What happens next:** the authors read every report and label the ones they accept. From then on the car shows **REPORTED** in everyone's app, and the fix comes in a later release, which the app installs itself.

<details>
<summary><b>Why does RVGL's box show other bars than the game?</b></summary>
<br>
The box shows what RVGL will show, worked out from the car's parameters in RVGL. For an RC Revenge or RC Revenge Pro car it comes from the game's own handling, whose bars in the game are set by hand; for a Gran Turismo 2 car from its top speed and 0-100 km/h in the game; for a Rollcage or Rollcage Stage II car from its runs in the game; for a car of the other games from the real car's top speed and 0-100 km/h, laid over RVGL's own cars. Its tooltip in the app says which.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What do «Under study» and «Verified» mean?</b></summary>
<br>
The dot beside a version tells how far along it is. <b>Verified</b> (green) means the car was checked against the game, by the authors and through users' reports. <b>Under study</b> (orange) means it is retuned to work in RVGL but not yet checked in full. Every car of the fourteen games added before v0.400 is Verified, and so is every car of the twelve games added in v0.400, from Destruction Derby to TOCA World Touring Cars; Gran Turismo 2 has 1,010 of its 1,043 versions Verified, and the other 33 keep small differences from the game.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where does the app keep its settings and crash reports?</b></summary>
<br>
In <code>settings.txt</code> of your application data folder (<code>%APPDATA%\rvgl-car-importer</code> on Windows, <code>~/.config/rvgl-car-importer</code> on Linux), the crash reports in its <code>crash</code> folder, the last 10.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do the exported cars go?</b></summary>
<br>
Into the <code>cars</code> folder you choose under <b>Export</b>, usually the one of your RVGL. «Delete» removes only cars this app exported.
</details>
